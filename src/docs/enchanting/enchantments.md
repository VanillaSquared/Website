## Dash

- **Slot:** Special
- **Maximum level:** III
- **Supported items:** Swords, axes and tridents

Press the **Enchantment Hotkey** while holding the weapon to launch yourself in the direction you are looking. Any mob hit during the dash takes 10 damage per level.

Higher levels give you a longer dash and another use before the cooldown starts. Dash I has one use, Dash II has two and Dash III has three. The cooldown is 4 seconds per level.

You cannot start a dash while you are in water, gliding with an elytra or riding another entity. You also need at least 7 food points unless you are in Creative Mode. Damage taken during the dash is increased, so dashing straight into a bad situation can very quickly backfire.

## Ruthless

- **Slot:** Damage
- **Maximum level:** I
- **Supported items:** Swords, axes and tridents

Ruthless multiplies the weapons attack damage by 1.5, but every successful attack also deals between 2 and 4 magic damage back to you.

The extra damage makes Ruthless useful when you want to end a fight quickly, but the self damage means you cannot just keep attacking without paying attention to your own health.

## Swirling

- **Slot:** Special
- **Maximum level:** I
- **Supported items:** Maces and spears

Press the **Enchantment Hotkey** to spin with the weapon for 4 seconds, dealing 6.5 damage to nearby mobs every 4 ticks in a 3.5 block radius.

While spinning, you are given Slowness IV for 10 seconds and Weakness II for 6 seconds. Swirling only has one level and one use, after which it goes on an 8 second cooldown.

## Void Strike

- **Slot:** Damage
- **Maximum level:** III
- **Supported items:** Melee weapons, maces, bows and crossbows

Every direct hit has a 25% chance to apply the **Voided** effect to the target.

The next time a voided target takes damage, that damage is increased and the effect is consumed. It starts at a 1.1 damage multiplier and becomes stronger while left on the target. The maximum multiplier depends on the enchantment level:

| Level | Effect duration | Maximum damage multiplier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | 5 seconds | 1.1 |
| II | 10 seconds | 1.2 |
| III | 15 seconds | 1.3 |

This means you can attack immediately for the smaller bonus or wait for the effect to build up before hitting the target again.

## Channeling

- **Slot:** Damage
- **Maximum level:** I
- **Supported items:** Swords, axes, tridents, bows and crossbows

Channeling is not a new enchantment, but Vanilla Squared completely changes how it works. It no longer needs a thunderstorm to do something.

Hitting a mob deals lightning damage and sends the lightning through up to 3 nearby mobs. The chain can travel up to 8 blocks through blocks included in the Channeling block tag. Bows and crossbows deal 6 to 8 lightning damage to the first target and 5 to 6 to every chained target. Swords, axes and tridents deal 4 to 6 to the first target and 3 to every chained target.

Channeling only has one level and is incompatible with Riptide.
