Ruthless is a damage enchantment which trades the users health for a large increase to weapon damage.

## Obtaining

The Ruthless Enchanting Recipe can be found in Dungeon chests, Stronghold library chests, Trial Chamber vaults and Desert or Snowy Librarian trades.

### Enchanting recipe

| Lapis | Skeleton Skull | Wither Roses | Sword | Axe | Chiseled Bookshelves | Levels |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 5 | 1 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 3 | 6 |

Both the Sword and Axe are recipe ingredients and are consumed. The item receiving Ruthless goes in the enchanting tables item slot and is not consumed.

## Usage

Ruthless multiplies the attacks final weapon damage by 1.5. After a successful hit, the attacker takes a random amount of magic damage between 2 and 4.

| Base attack damage | Damage with Ruthless |
| ---: | ---: |
| 4 | 6 |
| 6 | 9 |
| 8 | 12 |
| 10 | 15 |

The self damage ignores regular armor. Gold Armors magic protection can reduce it.

Ruthless is most useful when a small number of hits can finish the fight. Fast weapons do not reduce the self damage, so attacking more often also drains health more often.

## Supported items

Ruthless can be applied to Swords, Axes, Spears and Tridents. It uses one Damage slot and only works while the enchanted weapon is used from the main hand.
