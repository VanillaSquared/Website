Not every Vanilla Squared feature needs its own category. This page collects the other additions and changes which are still important to know about.

## Sulfur Cubes

Sulfur Cubes can be bred using Sulfur Goo. They can also swallow a Redstone Block or Sponge to turn into one of 2 new variants.

- The [Redstone Sulfur Cube](/docs/other-features/sulfur_cubes#redstone-sulfur-cube) is a moving redstone power source.
- The [Sponge Sulfur Cube](/docs/other-features/sulfur_cubes#sponge-sulfur-cube) removes water and dries itself in dimensions where water evaporates.

Read [Sulfur Cubes](/docs/other-features/sulfur_cubes) for their breeding, behavior and data values.

## Sulfur Spike Dripping

Downward facing Sulfur Spikes can drip Water and Lava into Cauldrons. Water dripping from them also speeds up Copper oxidization.

Read [Sulfur Spike Dripping](/docs/other-features/sulfur_spike_dripping) for how to build one and which blocks can be oxidized.
