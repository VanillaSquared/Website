Sulfur Cubes drop between 2 and 5 Sulfur Goo when killed. Adult Sulfur Cubes can also be fed Sulfur Goo to breed them.

## Gunpowder recipe

Combine the following items in any shape to craft 2 Gunpowder:

- 1 Coal or Charcoal
- 1 Bone Meal
- 1 Sulfur Goo

The recipe is unlocked as soon as you pick up Sulfur Goo.
